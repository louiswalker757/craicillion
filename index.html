About Craicillion

What is Craicillion?

Craicillion is a free daily word game for Ireland. Every day there's a new dive of 7 prompts, the same for everyone: name an Irish county, a packet of crisps, a DART stop, a GAA county nickname, a Leaving Cert subject and loads more. You get 25 seconds of air for each one.

Everyone says Dublin. The rarer your answer, the more points you score and the deeper Séamus the seal dives, from the Forty Foot past Selkie Waters and Manannán's Hall, all the way to Tír fo Thuinn, the land under the wave. It's like Wordle, but Irish, and the obvious answer won't get you far. Share your squares with the group chat and come back tomorrow for a new dive.

    How to play
    • Every day there's a new Daily Dive: 7 prompts, the same for everyone.
    • You get 25 seconds of air per prompt. Type one answer and hit Enter.
    • Everyone says Dublin. The rarer your answer, the more points it scores, and the deeper Séamus sinks.
    • Depth = points × 10 metres. Max is 700 points / 7,000 m.
    • Not on the list? Go again, but the air keeps draining. Run out of air = 0.
    • Fadas, capitals, typos, nicknames and half-names (“Sexton”, “Blarney”) are all grand.

    Landmarks on the way down

    One scored dive a day; it rolls over at midnight, Irish time. Infinite is endless practice: dive 7 prompts, surface, then keep going as long as you like. It doesn't touch your stats. Missed a day? Play any past dive from the Archive. And for a limited time, try Which is Rarer?: two answers from a past dive, tap the rarer one, and see how long a streak you can build. New: Real or Fake? shows you three Irish place names. Two are real and Séamus made one up. Spot the fake! And The Mini is a new 5×5 crossword every day, with Irish clues and English answers. Can you beat your mates' time?
